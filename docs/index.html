Chapter I · The Gate

A Week Has Its Pact

Weekyii is a local-first commitment system for iPhone and iPad. It gives every task a place in time, every day a moment of resolve, and every week a beginning and an end.

Week-driven Local-first iPhone + iPad Built in SwiftUI

“What enters the day must face the day.”

The Endless List Has No Consequences

Traditional task tools collect intentions without forcing a decision. Tasks drift across dates, unfinished work stays forever, and the list grows while the meaning of “today” dissolves.

Chapter III · The Oath

Chapter IV · The Time Engine

A Calendar That Advances as a State Machine

Future holds weeks that can be prepared without crowding the present. When their time arrives, they cross into Present.

At a daily deadline and at the weekly boundary, Weekyii closes unresolved states, updates the chronicle, and advances time. Past becomes an earned record, not a second inbox.

Chapter VI · The Daily Ritual

To Begin Is to Bind the Day

DraftArrange tasks while the day is still open.
ConfirmA two-stage ritual makes the decision explicit.
FocusOne task stands in the active zone.
AdvanceCompletion draws the next Frozen task forward.
CloseComplete the pact or meet the daily deadline.

A MindStamp message can accompany the start ritual, turning a utilitarian action into a personal threshold. The deadline remains visible, and the live state is shared with system surfaces.

Once execution begins, sequence matters: Weekyii protects attention from casual reordering while still recording the work that was truly completed.
