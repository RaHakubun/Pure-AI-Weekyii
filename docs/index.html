Chapter I · The Gate

Not More Tasks Managed.
This Week Completed.

Weekyii is not another endless to-do list. It is a local-first commitment system for iPhone and iPad: plan by week, make a pact with each day, face one task at a time, close every cycle, and preserve a record of the life you actually completed.

Week-Driven System Start Means Commit Entirely Local Data iPhone + iPad

“Planning is not collecting intentions. What enters today must face today.”

Chapter II · The Cracks

The Endless List Has No Consequences

Most task tools optimize for capturing more. Weekyii is built for finishing. When tasks can be postponed, copied and preserved at zero cost, a beautifully managed list can become the perfect place to avoid a decision.

01 · Endless Deferral

Tomorrow Costs Nothing

Tasks drift forward until planning becomes a warehouse of wishes. The list is managed; time is not.

02 · Endless Residue

Failure Follows Every New Day

03 · Endless Cycles

Effort Never Truly Closes

Without a daily ritual or weekly boundary, completion is merely a checkmark—not a record of a life lived.

Chapter III · The Oath

Weekyii’s Answer: Make Tasks Answer to Time

Ritual · Choose

Starting Turns Plans into a Pact

The draft remains free to change. Only starting Today moves its tasks into a deliberate stage of execution.

Focus · Protect Attention

Only One Task Faces You

Focus holds the present; Frozen guards what follows, turning “what should I do?” into one clear answer.

Forgetting · End the Debt

The Unfinished Does Not Rule Forever

At the daily deadline, unmet commitments expire. The past acknowledges the result without carrying failure intact into tomorrow.

This is not a decorative variation on the to-do list. It is a complete discipline of time: planning has boundaries, execution has order, failure has an ending, and only completion enters history.

Chapter IV · The Time Engine

Time Is Not a Label. It Is the Engine.

Future, Present and Past are not three manual lists. They are states advanced by real dates. The future may be prepared without crowding today; only when its time arrives does it become executable.

Daily deadlines and weekly boundaries close unresolved states, write the chronicle and advance the next cycle. Weekyii does not merely attach dates to tasks—it makes the entire task world obey time.

Chapter VI · The Daily Ritual

The Difference Begins After You Press Start

DraftArrange tasks while the day is still open.
ConfirmA two-stage ritual makes the decision explicit.
FocusOne task stands in the active zone.
AdvanceCompletion draws the next Frozen task forward.
CloseComplete the pact or meet the daily deadline.

A MindStamp message can accompany the start ritual, turning a utilitarian action into a personal threshold. The deadline remains visible, and the live state is shared with system surfaces.

Once execution begins, sequence matters: Weekyii protects attention from casual reordering while still recording the work that was truly completed.
