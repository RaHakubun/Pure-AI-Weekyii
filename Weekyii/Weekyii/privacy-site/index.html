Weekyii 隐私政策

Weekyii Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日　·　最近更新：2026 年 10 月 24 日

Effective: October 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 24, 2026

Weekyii 把隐私做得尽量简单：不需要账号，不收集个人数据，不投放广告，也不接入第三方分析工具。你的任务与图片默认只保存在设备上；只有当你主动开启 iCloud 同步时，它们才会进入你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库。

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、位置或广告标识符。
  • 不包含广告，也不集成第三方分析、归因或追踪 SDK。
  • iCloud 同步默认关闭，由你决定是否开启。
  • 购买由 Apple 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息。

Weekyii keeps privacy simple: no account, no collection of personal data, no ads, and no third-party analytics. Your tasks and images stay on your device by default. They reach your private iCloud database only if you choose to turn on iCloud sync.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs.
  • iCloud sync is off by default and only runs when you enable it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple, and we never receive your payment details.

01我们收集的信息

Weekyii 不收集可识别你身份的个人信息。应用不要求创建账号，也不会把你的任务内容上传到由开发者运营的服务器。

  • 不收集身份信息：不需要姓名、邮箱、手机号或账号。
  • 不收集设备标识：不读取广告标识符（IDFA），不进行跨应用追踪。
  • 不读取位置、通讯录、日历或健康数据。
  • 不使用第三方统计分析、广告、归因或行为画像服务。
  • 不收集你在应用内的操作记录用于营销。

01Information we collect

Weekyii does not collect personal information that identifies you. The app does not require an account, and your task content is never uploaded to a developer-operated server.

  • No identity information: no name, email address, phone number, or account.
  • No device identifiers: we do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across apps.
  • No access to location, contacts, calendar, or health data.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or profiling services.
  • No collection of in-app activity for marketing purposes.

02保存在你设备上的数据

你在 Weekyii 中创建的内容，包括周计划、今日任务、任务备注、子任务、完成记录、偏好设置，以及你主动添加的图片，默认保存在你的设备本地，仅用于向你展示和管理你的计划。

卸载应用会移除设备上的本地数据。由 iOS 系统备份（例如 iCloud 备份或电脑备份）保留的副本，受你的系统备份设置控制。

02Data stored on your device

Content you create in Weekyii — weekly plans, today's tasks, notes, subtasks, completion history, preferences, and any images you add — is stored locally on your device by default and used only to present and manage your plans.

Deleting the app removes local data from the device. Copies kept by iOS system backups (such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup) are governed by your backup settings.

03iCloud 同步

iCloud 同步默认关闭，只有在你于应用内主动开启后才会启用。开启后，Weekyii 通过 Apple CloudKit 将上述数据保存到你的 iCloud 私有数据库。

该数据库由你的 Apple ID 管理，开发者无法读取其中的内容。同步过程还受 Apple 的隐私政策与服务条款约束。你可以随时在应用内关闭同步，这不会影响设备上的本地数据；你也可以在 iOS「设置 → Apple ID → iCloud」中管理 Weekyii 的数据。

03iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default and only runs after you enable it in the app. Once enabled, Weekyii stores the data described above in your private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit.

That database is controlled by your Apple ID, and the developer cannot read its contents. Sync is also subject to Apple's privacy policy and terms of service. You can turn sync off at any time in the app without affecting local data on your device, and you can manage Weekyii's iCloud data in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

04照片

当你为任务添加图片时，Weekyii 会在获得你的许可后打开系统照片选择器，仅读取你主动选择的图片。图片会随任务保存在本地；若你开启 iCloud 同步，也会保存到你的 iCloud 私有数据库。

我们不会访问你未选择的照片，也不会将图片上传到开发者服务器或用于分析。

04Photos

When you attach an image to a task, Weekyii opens the system photo picker after you grant permission and reads only the images you choose. Images are stored locally with the task and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in your private iCloud database.

We do not access photos you did not select, upload images to a developer server, or use them for analysis.

05通知

若你允许通知，Weekyii 会使用 iOS 本地通知提醒你今日任务与截止时间。通知在你的设备上安排和触发，不经过开发者服务器。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭通知。

05Notifications

If you allow notifications, Weekyii uses local iOS notifications to remind you about today's tasks and deadlines. Notifications are scheduled and delivered on your device without passing through a developer server. You can turn them off at any time in system settings.

06应用内购买

Weekyii 的会员订阅与永久解锁通过 Apple App Store 的 StoreKit 完成。购买、续订、退款和支付方式均由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到或保存你的银行卡号、账单地址或其他支付信息。Apple 的隐私政策适用于这些交易。

应用会在设备上读取交易凭证以确认你的会员权益，不会将凭证发送给开发者服务器。你可以随时在 Apple ID 的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。

06In-app purchases

Weekyii memberships and the lifetime unlock are sold through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. Apple handles purchases, renewals, refunds, and payment methods. We never receive or store your card number, billing address, or other payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

The app reads transaction receipts on your device to confirm your membership. Receipts are not sent to a developer server. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID subscription settings.

07数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：在应用内删除任务或图片，或卸载应用即可移除。
  • iCloud 数据：关闭同步，或在 iOS 设置中删除 Weekyii 的 iCloud 数据。
  • 开发者服务器：我们不在自有服务器保存你的内容，因此没有需要另行申请删除的云端副本。

如需协助删除或对数据保留有疑问，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

07Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: delete tasks or images in the app, or remove the app from your device.
  • iCloud data: turn off sync, or delete Weekyii's iCloud data in iOS Settings.
  • Developer servers: we do not store your content on our own servers, so there is no additional cloud copy to request deletion for.

If you need help with deletion or have questions about retention, contact us at the email address below.

08儿童隐私

Weekyii 不是专为 13 岁以下儿童设计的产品，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。由于应用不要求账号、也不收集个人数据，正常使用不会产生此类信息。

如果你是家长或监护人，并认为孩子向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会尽快处理。

08Children's privacy

Weekyii is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app requires no account and collects no personal data, ordinary use does not create such information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email address below and we will address it promptly.

09第三方服务

Weekyii 仅使用 Apple 提供的系统服务：CloudKit（iCloud 同步）、StoreKit 与 App Store（购买），以及 iOS 通知（本地提醒）。应用不集成第三方广告、分析、归因或社交 SDK。

Apple 可能依据你的 iOS 设置收集诊断与崩溃信息，此类信息由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

09Third-party services

Weekyii uses only Apple system services: CloudKit for iCloud sync, StoreKit and the App Store for purchases, and iOS notifications for local reminders. The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or social SDKs.

Apple may collect diagnostics and crash information according to your iOS settings. That information is handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

10政策变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改「最近更新」日期。若变更会实质性影响你的权利，我们会在应用内以适当方式提示。

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects your rights, we will also provide an appropriate notice in the app.
